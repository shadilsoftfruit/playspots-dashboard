Imphal Dominates
1,409 of 1,784 bookings and 76.7% of all revenue comes from Imphal. The remaining 2 districts combined contribute just 375 bookings.

Q4 vs Q2 Growth
Q4 (Oct–Dec) delivered 697 bookings vs Q2's 292 — a 139% jump. October alone (278) was the highest single month, powered by Churachandpur's STAR7 ARENA launch.

186 packages offered vs 180 welcome offered. Churachandpur: perfect 100% conversion. Imphal: 164 offered vs 158 welcome offered, indicating carry-forward redemptions.

Chura Highest Ticket
Churachandpur earns ₹1,738 per booking — highest in the state — vs Imphal ₹1,447 and Thoubal ₹1,060. Premium facility mix drives higher value despite lower volume.

Thoubal averages ₹1,060 per booking — the lowest in the state. With 48 bookings and 8 playcoin uses, there is room to grow both volume and per-booking value through targeted promotions.
