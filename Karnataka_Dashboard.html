Key Stakeholder Insights
01
75.9%
Shivamogga Dominates
726 of 957 bookings come from Shivamogga — the dominant district. Dakshina Kannada (85) and Mangalapuram (68) round out the top 3, together accounting for 91.9% of state volume.
02
+50%
Q4 Peak Quarter
Q4 (Oct–Dec) was the strongest quarter at 307 bookings — 50% above Q1's 205. December alone was the peak month with 113 bookings, led by Shivamogga's 99-booking December.
03
105%
Pkg Conversion Rate
304 packages redeemed vs 289 welcome offered — a 105% conversion rate indicating healthy carry-forward redemptions. Shivamogga (227) and Mangalapuram (27) lead conversions across districts.
04
₹2,262
Mangalapuram Highest Ticket
Mangalapuram earns ₹2,262 per booking — highest in the state — well above the statewide average of ₹1,798. This reflects premium venue mix and higher-value bookings relative to district volume.
05
26.1%
Playcoin Penetration
250 of 957 bookings used playcoins (26.1%). Shivamogga leads with 196 playcoin bookings. Kolar and Raichur show near-zero adoption — a clear expansion opportunity for the loyalty program.
06
7
State District Coverage
Karnataka coverage spans 7 districts with highly concentrated distribution. Top 3 districts (Shivamogga, Dakshina Kannada, Mangalapuram) account for 91.9% of all bookings. Kolar, Raichur, and Uttara Kannada are nascent — primed for venue expansion.
