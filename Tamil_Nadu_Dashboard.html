Key Stakeholder Insights
01
15.2%
Kanyakumari Leads
2,454 of 16,196 bookings come from Kanyakumari — the top district. Tirunelveli (1,729) and Pondicherry (1,231) round out the top 3, together accounting for 33.5% of state volume.
02
+84%
Q2 Peak vs Q1
Q2 (Apr–Jun) was the strongest quarter at 4,971 bookings — 84% above Q1's 2,703. June alone was the highest month statewide, driven by peak summer sporting activity across Kanyakumari and Thiruvarur.
03
119%
Pkg Over-Redemption
4,098 packages redeemed vs 3,454 offered — a 119% conversion rate indicating strong carry-forward redemptions. Kanyakumari (124%) and Vellore (125%) lead conversion across districts.
04
₹1,753
Pondicherry Highest Ticket
Pondicherry earns ₹1,753 per booking — highest in the state — well above the statewide average of ₹1,393. This reflects premium venue mix and higher engagement from tourist-adjacent demand.
05
18.2%
Playcoin Penetration
2,947 of 16,196 bookings used playcoins (18.2%). Kanyakumari leads with 443 playcoin bookings. Several newer districts show near-zero adoption — a clear expansion opportunity for the loyalty program.
06
31
State-Wide Coverage
Tamil Nadu coverage spans 31 districts with highly uneven distribution. Top 5 districts account for 48% of all bookings. Districts like Ramnathapuram, Thanjavur, and Tiruchirapalli are nascent — primed for venue expansion.
